# Review C-5: synthetic keep-alive

Verdict: **APPROVED**

Reproduced: ruff, mypy, pytest clean (existing synthetic tests unchanged, 4 new keep-alive tests). Persistent connections loop until client close, `Connection: close` or 30 s idle; malformed requests and handler errors close only their own connection; service time and semaphore semantics unchanged.
Measured ceiling (worker, real k6, target on 1 CPU, 1 ms service time): at least 1200 req/s with 0 dropped iterations at every tested rate (100-1200); the ceiling itself was not reached. That is far above the largest validation capacity (200 req/s).
Caveat from the worker's table: p99 was noisy and non-monotonic (67 ms at 100 req/s vs 3.5 ms at 1200 req/s at 1 ms service time), probably cold-start/connection ramp. The knee criterion uses the lowest step's P99 as baseline, so a noisy baseline is a validation risk; watch it in the real run.
