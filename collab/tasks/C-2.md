## Scope
- `assign_cpusets(total_cpus, sut_cpus, load_cpus) -> CpuAssignment`: disjoint sets, validates no overlap and no over-allocation, reserves nothing silently.
- `render_compose(spec, assignment, image_digests) -> dict` producing a compose document: SUT service(s) and k6 service with `cpuset`, images referenced by digest (`image@sha256:...`), healthchecks, a dedicated network.
- Pure functions; no Docker calls. Output written by the runner (L-3).

## Out of scope
Starting containers. Postgres/Redis services (Week 4+).
