## Scope
`serverlab overhead <results_dir>` analyses one finished experiment whose conditions are exactly `collector-off` and `collector-on` (docs/phases/week2.md s3, s6.3). It reads `spec.yaml` and `raw/run_<cond>_<n>.json`, computes `utilization_pct = 100 * sut_cpu_usec / (duration_s * 1e6 * len(cpus.sut))` per included run, prints min/median/max per condition, median p99 per condition, `overhead_pct_of_sut_capacity = median(on) - median(off)`, and a verdict against 2%. Runs with `result.valid` false or `sut_cpu_usec` null are listed with the reason and excluded. Fewer than 3 included runs per condition prints INSUFFICIENT DATA and exits 3. Bad input exits 2.
